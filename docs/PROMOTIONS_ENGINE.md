# NextStore Promotional Coupons & Discount Engine

Supports percentage-based, flat rupee, and threshold-gated discounts.

## FLAT50 Coupon
Flat ₹50 discount applied on minimum order value of ₹300.

## SAVE10 Coupon
10% discount on entire bill subtotal.

## NEXTSTORE20 Coupon
20% celebratory discount for new store launch.

## SUPERMCA Coupon
Special student educational discount for academic demonstrations.

## Coupon Pill
Applied coupon appears with green checkmark and 'Remove' button in order summary.
